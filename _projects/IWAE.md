description: Reproduced and validated the Importance Weighted Autoencoder (IWAE) results from Burda et al., investigating a novel loss function with strictly tighter lower bounds than traditional VAEs. Implemented and compared both VAE and IWAE architectures on MNIST and Omniglot datasets, analyzing negative log-likelihood improvements with varying numbers of Monte Carlo samples. Results successfully confirmed the original findings, showing clear performance gains for larger sample counts and demonstrating IWAE's ability to learn richer latent space representations through its relaxed assumptions.
